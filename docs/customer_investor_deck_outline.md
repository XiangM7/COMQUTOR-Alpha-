# Customer and Investor Deck Outline

## 1. Problem

Core: multi-agent research produces volume, not a dependable decision structure.

- Long narratives are hard to compare.
- Repeated claims can look like independent confirmation.
- Disagreement is buried in prose.
- Evidence provenance is easy to lose.

Visual: raw report fragments converging into an overloaded summary. Speaker note: frame the
auditability problem, not investment performance.

## 2. Why Ordinary Multi-Agent Summaries Fail

Core: vote counts confuse agent identity with economic independence.

- Agents can repeat the same underlying factor.
- A final summary can erase minority risk evidence.
- Narrative ranking is difficult to reproduce.
- Raw model output is unsafe as a customer contract.

Visual: five agents repeating two economic drivers. Speaker note: explain merge-by-structure.

## 3. COMQUTOR in One Line

Core: COMQUTOR turns agent research into traceable Alpha structures and deterministic conflicts.

- Structured claims
- Taxonomy-bounded mapping
- Versioned activation
- Evidence-linked conflict readout

Visual: claim → Alpha → graph → conflict radar. Speaker note: local Research MVP boundary.

## 4. Structured Claim Pipeline

Core: every usable assertion becomes a bounded, addressable record.

- Stable run, claim and source IDs
- Claim/evidence separation

- No raw Provider response in the public readout

Visual: one structured claim record. Speaker note: emphasize traceability and safe defaults.

## 5. Alpha Taxonomy

Visual: selected taxonomy branches. Speaker note: no ad hoc labels are promoted to facts.
